OUR HERITAGE

Brief History of Eagles Academy Aso Pada (EAAP)

The inspiration to found Eagles Academy Aso Pada came in 2012 when the Executive Director, Dr. Alex A. Maiyanga mni, and the Proprietress, Mrs. Victoria J. Maiyanga, saw the need to establish a world-class institution that would deliver the best standard of education to the Nigerian child at the sub-degree level.

The school started its operations in August 2013 with a ‘summer school’ which lasted for a month. Following that, the school was dedicated on Thursday, 5th September 2013 by Pastor Gideon Oyedepo, a Senior Pastor of Living Faith Church (AKA Winners’ Chapel), Life Camp, Abuja.

Monday, 9th September 2013 brought a new dawn in private education social enterprise in Nigeria. Thus, full academic activities flagged off with state-of-the-art facilities for teaching/learning activities in Aso Pada, Nasarawa State. From day one, the library, staff offices, furniture, playground, and a computer laboratory were readily on ground.

Pioneer Academic Staff

Mrs. Oluwakemi A. Makinwa (Pioneer Head Facilitator), Mr. Stephen Egbadekwu (Pioneer Assistant Head Facilitator), Mr. Abraham A. Adebote, Miss Mary A. Adeyinka, Mrs. Vera Ozoilo, and Miss Moses Patience Ako.

On the first day of resumption, eleven (11) learners were enrolled. However, Eagles Academy Aso Pada enrolled twenty-eight (28) learners at the end of the First Term 2013/2014 Academic Session.

Presently, Eagles Academy Aso Pada has grown rapidly through the help of Almighty God and the support of her members of staff. In September 2016, after three years of intense academic work, the Junior Secondary School started off with our first set of learners who were enrolled at the time of establishment. The secondary school learners and the pioneering staff didn’t relent in their efforts as the students were ever ready to face challenges that come with college life.

Not resting on her oars, Eagles Academy Aso Pada added another feather to its cap by establishing a full-fledged secondary school whose building was completed in 2020. The secondary school building is a masterpiece of architectural design which is fully furnished with laboratories for Physics, Biology, Chemistry, and Agricultural Sciences. The computer laboratory is also equipped with computer pieces that give all students hands-on experience.

Further advancements were recorded when in November 2022, Eagles Academy Aso Pada was fully accredited and registered as WAEC and NECO Examination Centers for the senior secondary school cadre. All these garlands were achieved within the first decade of the life of Eagles Academy Aso Pada to the glory of the giver of all good gifts, God the creator.

DIRECTOR'S NOTE

From the Executive Director

Dear all,

I welcome you all to our site with unquantifiable humility and unquenchable gratitude to the creator.

Eagles Academy Aso Pada is founded on the belief that in this wide world, one can put a dot in it and it will be ascribed to one in posterity. As Neil Armstrong was quoted to have said, “It is one small step for a man and giant leap for mankind”; so is our little step in social entrepreneurship in education. Therefore, Eagles Academy Aso Pada is a small step that will impact our world through our learners.

The Nigerian education is in shambles and we needed to change the narrative by establishing a world-class institution at the sub-degree level. Eagles Academy is therefore a model in the provisions of infrastructure and in the quality of our staffers. To us, quality is not an act but a habit, a culture.

Along with known philosopher kings, we desire to be the change we wish to see in education in Nigeria. According to Aristotle, it is a mark of an educated mind to be able to entertain thought without accepting it. Thus, we say that we do not accept the way education is handled in Nigeria and we have the onus to change the direction by establishing a model institution called Eagles Academy Aso Pada.

Our vision for the future is the deployment of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) in our classroom facilitations, deliveries, and interactions. Barring our location, all our facilitators are made to have hands-on experience in computer applications for classroom deliveries.

Our gratitude is to GOD who ordered our steps packaged with wisdom to contribute a little to the challenge of unemployment in our country. We are thankful to our parents/guardians and the host community for their wonderful support. The academic and administrative staffers deserve a special place in my mind.

DR. ALEX A. MAIYANGA (mni)

NCE; BA (Hons); MA; M.Phil; PhD; rtn
