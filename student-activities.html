Story Time: Wise Advice

Ramu and Shamu were good friends. Both of them were woodcutters who stayed near the woods. One day, both of them went to the woods to cut trees. Shamu began to boast about his bravery and told Ramu, “I am not afraid of wild animals. I can kill them with one stroke of my sharp axe!”

Ramu heard this and said, “That’s very brave! I am proud to have a friend like you.”

Suddenly, they saw a big black bear coming towards them. On seeing the bear, Shamu got so scared that he ran and climbed the nearest tree, leaving Ramu alone.

Ramu did not know how to climb a tree. He thought, “Now what should I do? I have heard that bears never touch a dead person. I’ll try and save my life.” As the bear approached fast, Ramu lay down on the ground pretending to be dead, holding his breath and keeping as still as a corpse.

The bear circled around him, smelt him, and thinking Ramu was dead, walked away into the woods. After the bear went away, Shamu came down from the tree and asked, “Hey Ramu, I saw the bear whisper something in your ear. What did he say?”

Ramu answered: "The bear gave me very wise advice. He told me to never trust a friend who boasts about himself, for he shall leave you in times of difficulty."

Shamu was ashamed of himself and realized his mistake.

The Missing Test Paper

A teacher popped out of the classroom, leaving a test paper on her desk. When she came back, it was missing! Four students were in the room: Ku, Lu, Mu, and Nu. Of these, only one tells the truth.

  • Ku said: "Mu took it."
  • Lu said: "I didn't take it."
  • Mu said: "Lu is lying."
  • Nu said: "Mu is lying."
Who took the test paper?
Answer: Lu took the test paper!

Riddles & Mind Teasers

1. Mr. and Mrs. Mustard have six daughters and each daughter has one brother. How many people are in the Mustard family?

There are 9 Mustards in the family (6 daughters, 1 brother shared by all, and Mr. & Mrs. Mustard).

2. I am something people love or hate. I change people's appearance and thoughts. What am I?

Answer: Age

3. Only one color but not one size, stuck at the bottom yet easily flies. Present in Sun but not in rain. Doing no harm and feeling no pain. What am I?

Answer: A Shadow

4. Who is that with a neck and no head, two arms and no hands?

Answer: A Shirt

5. If eleven plus two equals one, what does nine plus five equal?

Answer: 2 O'clock (11 o'clock + 2 hrs = 1 o'clock; 9 o'clock + 5 hrs = 2 o'clock).

6. Can you write down eight eights so that they add up to one thousand?

Answer: 888 + 88 + 8 + 8 + 8 = 1,000

7. What English word retains the same pronunciation even after you take away five letters?

Answer: Queue (Remove 'u-e-u-e' and it is still pronounced 'Q').
