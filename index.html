Facts About Eagles

Eagles are some of the largest birds. They are at the top of the food chain, with some species feeding on big prey like Monkeys and Sloths. Eagles have amazing eyesight and can detect prey up to two miles away.

An Eagle may resemble a vulture in flight characteristics but has a fully feathered (often crested) head, strong feet equipped with great curved talons. A further difference is in foreign habits. Eagles feed mainly on live prey.

An eagle is a symbol of freedom and peace. Our burnished learners will come out strongest academically, embodying the resilience, vision, and strength of the eagle!
